# Product vision

StateHubs is an international real-estate CRM/ERP for independent agents, agencies and eventually multi-office organizations. Its core outcome is less administrative work and more completed sales/rentals. English is the default/fallback language; Spain, US, UK, UAE, Portugal, France, Italy and international tourist markets guide localization, not hard-coded domain assumptions.

The daily product joins opportunities, people, properties, appointments, tasks, transactions and rentals. A lead is an opportunity before an agency-defined commercial conversion; a client relationship can carry several roles. A phone call alone is not conversion. Preserve identity, activities and origin through every transition.

Build on existing blueprints, working views, private media, local/server API contracts and versioned proposals. The browser demo remains explicitly identified. Production readiness additionally requires real authentication, permission tests, validated writes, recovery, observability and verified deployment.

Delivery order: critical correctness/security; coherent working CRM shell; leads/clients/pipeline; property and rental operations; complete localization; verified integrations/automation; commercial administration. [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) supplies acceptance gates and [FEATURE_MATRIX](FEATURE_MATRIX.md) distinguishes present code from planned work.

Starter, Professional, Business and Enterprise are candidate plan names. Prices, limits and billing provider are not decided. Do not invent them or incur external costs. No claim of complete legal compliance or valid electronic signature is supported today.
