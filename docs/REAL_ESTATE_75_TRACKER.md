## Verification requirements
- Implemented requires verified behavior, durable storage, authorized multi-tenant access, regression tests and documented UX.
- Live requires a verified production deployment and any necessary external provider activation.
- Never count domain types, configuration cards or mock data as delivered services.
- All product UI and document template text is English-first; preserve legacy internal entity keys.
- Legal documents require local counsel-approved templates and human review; providers require verified coverage and consent.

## Delivery sequence
1. Merge and verify existing branches, fix failing CI, preserve all existing features.
2. Finish reliable tenant-safe CRUD, reservation overlap prevention and shared contact identity.
3. Consolidate card-first navigation, role dashboards, English localization and multi-module persistence.
4. Complete brokerage, residential lease and hospitality operations before adding new verticals.
5. Add investments, development, commissions and audited intelligence.
6. Add work-order persistence, provider accounts, dispatch and inspections.
7. Add jurisdiction-aware document preparation with professional review and signature integrations.
8. Pilot selected countries and providers, then scale after operational verification.
